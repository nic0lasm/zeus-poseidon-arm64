Skeleton letters for the Ukrainian alphabet in the fonts of Zeus + Poseidon.

They were drawn for this project, one pixel wide, in the style of the game's own "Greek" fonts:
they are not game data. tools/alphabet.py turns each skeleton into a glyph of every font of the
player's own copy of the game, taking the colours from that font's Latin letters.

Three sizes (sets):
  A  capitals of the 11 pt fonts, up to 13 rows; doubled for the capitals of the 22 pt fonts
  B  small capitals of the 11 pt fonts, capitals of the 9 pt fonts and of SPR_FONTS (9 rows);
     doubled for the small capitals of the 22 pt fonts
  C  small letters of the 9 pt fonts, of SPR_FONTS_DKMESSAGE and of SPR_FONTS (6 to 7 rows)
The small letters of these fonts are small capitals, so a lower-case letter takes the skeleton
of its capital from the smaller set.

Syntax: everything before the first "==" line is this note. "== SET LETTER" starts a skeleton;
its rows follow, "#" for a pixel of the stroke and "." for none. The last row before a line
holding only "-" is the baseline; rows after it go below the line (Д, Ц, Щ). Marks above a
letter (Й, Ї, Ґ) are simply extra rows on top: the glyph is lifted by itself (anchor y).
