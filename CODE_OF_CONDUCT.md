# Code of conduct

This project brings together people who love an old game. Keep it that way.

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are players, not programmers, and English or French
  may not be their first language.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Do not share or ask for copies of the game, serial numbers or ways around copy protection.

A message that breaks these rules may be edited or removed, and its author blocked from the
repository. To report a problem, open a discussion or contact the maintainer through GitHub
(@nic0lasm).
