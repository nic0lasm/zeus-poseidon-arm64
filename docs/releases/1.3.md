## What is new: saves without a pause, Intel Macs and Linux

- **Saves without a pause.** Every save, by hand or the monthly autosave, froze the game while the emulated original compressed it: 3.85 s for the large city of the last tutorial. The compressor (PKWARE DCL implode) is now rewritten by hand in native code: the same save takes **0.33 s**, and the saved files are identical to the original's, byte for byte ([#122](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/pull/122), fixes [#116](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/issues/116)).
- **Intel Macs.** A second archive, built and self-tested by the CI on an Intel Mac of GitHub (macOS 15 or later) ([#115](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/pull/115), fixes [#41](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/issues/41)).
- **Linux.** A single AppImage file for x86-64 or arm64, about 5 MB, tested by the CI in a bare Debian 12 ([#119](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/pull/119), fixes [#40](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/issues/40)). Make it executable (`chmod +x`), then run it and pick your GOG installer.
- **Lighter interface.** Every one-line text of the game (panel titles and values, buttons, menus) is now drawn in native code: 7 to 9 % less CPU in real play on the large city ([#113](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/pull/113)).
- **A new home page** for the repository, in English and French, with a download table by computer and the gains of each version ([#118](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/pull/118)).

## How it is proven

- **Ten replayable scenarios**: every frame and the whole game state, at every simulation tick, compared with the pure original ([equivalence.md](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/blob/main/docs/equivalence.md)).
- **Saves:** fourteen test saves, two and three saves in a row, identical with and without native code; every call of the compressor checked call by call, blocks too random to compress included.

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## First launch on a Mac

1. Unzip the archive and double-click **Zeus Poseidon.app**.
2. macOS refuses to open it, because the application is not notarized by Apple. Open *System Settings → Privacy & Security*, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**, then confirm.
3. Pick your GOG installer (`setup_zeus_and_poseidon_….exe`). It is unpacked once, then the game starts.

## Checked

- Played on a Mac before release.
- The ten scenarios identical to the original at every tick, on every change.
- Every archive and AppImage is built by the CI: self-test, and a search of every file for names that must not be published. None contains a game file.

Please report anything odd in an [issue](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/issues).
