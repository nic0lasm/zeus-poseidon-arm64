**Download:**
- `Zeus-Poseidon-macos-arm64.zip`: Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later.
- `Zeus-Poseidon-macos-intel.zip`: Mac with an Intel processor, macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later.

## What is new

Everything in this version was contributed by [@northdakota](https://github.com/northdakota): thank you!

- **Smoother motion.** The game showed a new picture only every third simulation tick, so walkers jumped three steps at a time. There is now a picture for every tick, up to about 60 per second ([#155](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/pull/155)).
- **Smooth scrolling of the city.** The map no longer jumps a whole tile at a time ([#152](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/pull/152)).
- **Keyboard scrolling.** W A S D (Z Q S D on an AZERTY keyboard) scroll the city like the arrows, held keys scroll steadily, and holding a letter no longer opens macOS's accent menu ([#151](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/pull/151)).
- **The game's own cursors**: the shovel, the sword, the magnifier and the game's arrow, sharp on a Retina screen ([#149](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/pull/149)).
- **Menus and world map centred in wide modes.** In a mode larger than 1024 × 768, the menus' backgrounds were shifted and cut, so that the buttons no longer matched them ([#159](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/issues/159)), and the city showed around the world map. Both are now centred ([#148](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/pull/148), [#150](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/pull/150)).
- **Steam edition found in Wine bottles.** On a Mac, the Steam edition installed with CrossOver, Whisky or Wine is found without pointing to its folder ([#153](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/pull/153)).
- **Translating into a new language, another alphabet included.** `tools/translate.py` puts the game's texts into PO files and builds a pack from them; a pack can now bring letters the original fonts do not have, such as Cyrillic, drawn with `tools/alphabet.py`. A Ukrainian example is in `tools/examples/lang/uk` ([#158](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/pull/158), [#24](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/issues/24)).
- **Fonts documented** for translators and modders: `formats/fonts.md` and `tools/fonts.py` ([#157](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/pull/157)).

Smooth motion and scrolling can be turned off with `ZEUS_FRAME_PACING=0` and `ZEUS_SMOOTH_SCROLL=0`. The game's own code is unchanged (a pack without new letters reads exactly as before), and each change was checked against the ten reference scenarios.

## First launch

- **Mac:** unzip, double-click **Zeus Poseidon.app**. The application is not notarized by Apple: open *System Settings → Privacy & Security* (on macOS 11 and 12: *System Preferences → Security & Privacy → General*), click **Open Anyway**, then pick your GOG installer or game folder.
- **Linux:** make the AppImage executable (`chmod +x`), run it.

Every archive and AppImage is built and self-tested by the CI; none contains a game file. Please report anything odd in an [issue](https://github.com/nic0lasm/zeus-poseidon-arm64/issues).
